Bullying Support & Coping Guide

Practical steps to protect yourself and get help safely.

What to do if you are Verbal/School Bullied
  • Stay Calm & Walk Away: Bullies seek a reactive emotional response.
  • Keep a Record: Write down dates, places, and names of people involved.
  • Tell a Trusted Adult: Inform a parent, teacher, or counselor right away.
  • Use HearME Anonymous Chat: Talk through your feelings before deciding next steps.
How to Handle Cyberbullying Online
  • Do Not Reply: Responding often escalates online harassment.
  • Take Screenshots: Capture evidence before messages or posts can be deleted.
  • Block & Report: Use app block buttons and report toxic accounts immediately.
  • Set Accounts to Private: Restrict who can comment or message you.
